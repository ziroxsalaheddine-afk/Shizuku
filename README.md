# Shizuku — Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

## Terms of Service
By adding or using **Shizuku**, a temporary voice channel (TempVoice) Discord bot, you agree to use the bot in accordance with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Do not abuse voice room controls, permissions, or bot commands. Features and availability may change or be updated at any time.

## Privacy Policy
- **Data Collected:** Shizuku processes Discord user IDs, guild/channel IDs, and voice state/permission settings required to create and manage temporary voice channels and apply requested controls (such as lock, unlock, limit, rename, permit, or reject).
- **Message Content:** Message content is processed only when executing prefix commands/arguments and is not sold or used to train machine learning or AI models.
- **Data Storage & Security:** Any stored configuration data (such as TempVoice setup options) is kept only as needed for bot functionality, protected with standard security/encryption practices, and never sold to third parties.
- **Data Deletion Requests:** Server owners or users may request deletion of their data by contacting the developer through the support server or contact channels.
